# Solution: LP-0002 - LEZ-Quorum Private Threshold Treasury

**Submitted by:** FidelCoder

## Summary

Quorum is a private M-of-N treasury for LEZ v0.2.2. Members prove credential
control and Merkle membership without publishing their identities. The chain
stores proposal-bound nullifiers and approval counts.

## Approach

The constitution stores a Merkle root over LEZ private-account credentials.
Each approval proves credential control, membership, and a unique
proposal-bound nullifier in Risc0. The SPEL gate verifies the receipt and
executes the action after the required approvals.

The SDK, CLI, and Basecamp module build the LEZ transaction. Public writes are
previewed and saved before submission.
