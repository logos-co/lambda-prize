# Solution: LP-0002 - LEZ-Quorum Private Threshold Treasury

**Submitted by:** FidelCoder

## Summary

Quorum is a private M-of-N treasury for LEZ v0.2.2. Members prove credential
control and Merkle membership locally. The chain records proposal-bound
nullifiers and approval counts, not member identities or credentials.

## Approach

The constitution stores a Merkle root over LEZ private-account credentials.
Each approval proves credential control, active membership, and a unique
proposal-bound nullifier in Risc0. The SPEL gate verifies the receipt, rejects
duplicate votes, enforces treasury policy, and executes only after threshold.

The SDK, CLI, and Basecamp app compose the gate receipt inside an LEZ private
transaction. Public writes are journaled, previewed, explicitly approved, and
reconciled by exact transaction hash and bytes.
