# LP-0028: Sustained Atomic-Swap Maker Operation on the Logos testnet [DRAFT]

**`Status: Draft`**
**`Logos Circle: N/A`**

## Overview

This prize pays an independent operator for **keeping a BTC↔LEZ atomic-swap maker live on the official Logos LEZ testnet for 90 consecutive days**, serving real, unaffiliated takers with executable offers priced against a published reference. Where LP-0027 proves that strangers can stand a maker up, this prize proves that one can be run as a service: continuously, at a stated quality, for people who are not the operator.

It is a testnet prize by design. Logos mainnet is not live, RFP-003 delivers and tests on test networks only, and the atomic-swap protocol has not had its independent review. When mainnet and the M7 review exist, a separate mainnet liquidity prize will replace this one with value-at-risk criteria.

## Motivation

A swap network needs makers who stay. Short observation windows show that a kit works; they do not show that anyone will keep inventory funded, offers fresh and a host healthy through three months of testnet resets, chain stalls and quiet weeks with no takers. That operational habit is what a later mainnet market needs, and it is cheaper to learn on a faucet testnet than with real funds.

Paying for operation rather than code is unusual for a λPrize, so the criteria below are written to be checked from outside: an external offer probe, both-chain swap records, and takers whose independence can be inspected. Nothing depends on the operator's own reporting.

## Success Criteria

### Functionality

- [ ] The maker runs a **pinned, unmodified RFP-003 release** (tag, commit, digest recorded at registration), on a host the operator controls, with keys the operator holds. Running the LP-0027 winning kit is expected but not required; an operator using their own tooling must meet the same criteria.
- [ ] **Executable offers** for BTC↔LEZ in **both directions** are published on Logos Delivery at or above a minimum lot size set at opening, with a TTL, and are honoured when taken: a taker following the published Taker desk or CLI completes or is refunded per protocol with no operator intervention.
- [ ] **Price quality**: each live offer's ratio is within a **band around the testnet reference ratio stated in this prize at opening** (the ratio, the band and the rule for changing the ratio are all written into this file when the prize opens). Testnet tokens carry no market price, so the reference is a fixed number for evaluation only, not a price feed. The operator's policy may read it from the release's price-source mechanism or an external adapter.
- [ ] **Second pair, conditional**: if RFP-003 has delivered XMR↔LEZ or ZEC↔LEZ to public test networks before this prize opens, the same criteria apply to one additional pair. Otherwise the second pair is reflected favourably, not required.

### Reliability

- [ ] Restarts, host reboots, Bitcoin backend outages and Logos Delivery or Chat outages are recovered without loss of funds and without a taker's swap being left unresolvable.
- [ ] Every swap that fails on the operator's side is either refunded per protocol or recorded with an evidence export and a cause.

### Supportability

- [ ] The operator publishes a short **operations log** at the end of the window: incidents, recoveries, upgrades, and the issues filed upstream or on the kit, each with a link.
- [ ] Any friction that blocks other operators is filed on the kit or upstream repository rather than worked around silently.

### Adoption

> These metrics measure traction attributable to a given submission for this prize.
>
> **All of the following are required in addition to the criteria above.** Evaluators will inspect hosts, keys, taker accounts and posting histories. Volume that is clearly manufactured does not count, regardless of the headline number.

- [ ] **90 consecutive days of operation**, starting only after registration with Logos of the operator, the maker's Delivery identity, the host and the release pin. Days before registration do not count.
- [ ] **Offer availability of at least 95 %** over the window, measured by an external probe run by Logos reading Logos Delivery. Declared platform-wide outages pause the clock; operator-side outages do not.
- [ ] **At least 50 settled swaps** with both-chain evidence, from **at least 15 independent takers**: distinct people or organisations, none affiliated with the operator, Logos or a Logos CC, each with prior unrelated testnet activity. Takers created in a burst around the window, or funded from the operator's wallets, do not count. Evaluators will inspect taker accounts and funding flows and will contact a sample of takers directly.
- [ ] **Spread over the window**: at least 10 settled swaps in each 30-day third of the window. A single burst does not qualify, however large.
- [ ] **Price quality held**: no more than 5 % of probe samples show an offer outside the reference band.
- [ ] **Zero unrecovered incidents**: no taker left with funds locked beyond the protocol's refund path, and no operator loss attributable to operation rather than to an upstream defect that was reported.
- [ ] (Discretionary) Takers' written feedback on offer quality and swap experience; evaluators will contact a sample directly.

**Not counted.** Stars, forks, follower counts, impressions, likes and reposts, signups, download counts and site visits are not adoption evidence. Neither are swaps between the operator and itself, its affiliates, another registered operator, the submitting team of LP-0027, Logos or a Logos CC. Testnet volume in units has no value and is not itself a criterion; only the counts, takers, availability and price quality above are.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Running, funding from public faucets, monitoring and recovering one maker for the window.
- Publishing the operations log and the evidence the criteria require.

### Out of Scope

- Building the maker, kit, price adapters or desks. Those are RFP-003 and LP-0027 deliverables.
- Mainnet, real-value inventory, exchange price feeds, hedging and any measure of economic liquidity. These belong to a future mainnet prize gated on Logos mainnet and the RFP-003 M7 review.
- Program-funded inventory or capital. Testnet funds come from the public faucets.
- Security review of the protocol or release.

Open to any individual or organisation that is not a Logos CC (including the RFP-003 contractor and its sub-contractors), not the LP-0027 winning team, and not affiliated with either. Participants operate as principals in their own name and are solely responsible for any legal, licensing or regulatory obligation that applies to them for running such a service, even on a test network (see [Terms & Conditions](../TERMS.md)).

## Submission Requirements

- Registration record (operator, Delivery identity, host, release pin, start date) filed with Logos before the window.
- The Logos probe report for availability and price quality.
- Both-chain evidence for the qualifying swaps, exported with the release's tooling, with taker identities as recorded at swap time.
- The operations log with linked issues.
- A solution PR in this repository summarising the above with links; no code deliverable is required.

## Evaluation Process

Registrations are accepted first-come-first-served once the prize is open. A registration cannot qualify before its 90-day window has elapsed. Logos runs the external offer probe for every registered operator; Logos does not take swaps, hold funds, or operate any maker. Evaluators will contact takers directly and will inspect taker accounts, hosts and funding flows for independence.

The following policies apply to all prizes (see [evaluation policies](../README.md#evaluation-policies)):

- **Submissions:** each operator is allowed one registration at a time and a maximum of **3 submissions** for this prize.
- **Feedback:** initial evaluation feedback is limited to a pass/fail indication against the success criteria.

**Opening condition.** This prize opens only after LP-0027 has a winning submission, the reference ratio, band and probe method have been written into this file, and the official LEZ testnet is expected to remain stable for a 90-day window. It stays in Draft until then.
